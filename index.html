About the project

A low-resource neural machine translation system for two Catanduanes Bikol varieties: Virac (Southern) and Pandan (Northern).

How it works

The translator is NLLB-200 (distilled, 600M parameters) fine-tuned with LoRA on a small parallel corpus. It translates English to Virac, English to Pandan, and Virac to Pandan.

A translation memory answers sentences it already knows, and new sentences can be added without retraining. When a sentence is not close to anything it has learned, it answers mai ko isi (Pandan) or dai ko isi (Virac) instead of guessing.

Good to know

The training data covers cooking and food sentences only, so other topics may be translated poorly. Use the report button to flag mistakes.
